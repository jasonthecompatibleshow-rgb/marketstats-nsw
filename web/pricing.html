Pricing FAQ

Questions

Why is the core map free?

MarketStats is built on open government data, which is free to reuse with attribution. We think the core numbers (rents, prices, yields, trends and the calculator) should be free for everyone. Paid plans are for tools that save time, such as tracking, alerts, exports and reports.

Why do I need an account for the free data?

You can browse the headline map, top-5 movers and NSW housing context without one. A free account (just an email) unlocks the deeper data: full history, bedroom splits, the full calculator and complete rankings. It lets us send you data-release updates if you want them and keeps the service sustainable. Right now the site is a preview in demo mode, so accounts are stored only in your browser. See our privacy policy.

Do prices include GST? Are they final?

Prices are in Australian dollars and are indicative until paid plans launch. Final prices will include GST where it applies, and we'll confirm them before you're ever charged.

Can I pay today?

No. Investor and Pro are coming soon. Nothing can be bought yet. The waitlist form on this page is a placeholder that doesn't send or store anything.Join the waitlist and we'll email you when they open.

Where does the data come from?

Official public datasets: the NSW Department of Communities and Justice Rent and Sales Report, the Australian Bureau of Statistics, the Reserve Bank of Australia, APRA and the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare. Every plan uses the same data. See Data & method for sources, licences and attribution.

Will I be able to cancel?

Yes. When paid plans launch, you'll be able to cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Annual plans will work the same way. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law always apply.

Is this financial advice?

No. All plans provide general information only, not financial, tax or investment advice. Gearing and cash-flow figures are modelled estimates. Read the disclaimer.
