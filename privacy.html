Right now: preview in demo mode

This version of MarketStats is a preview running in demo mode. When you "create an account" or "sign in", your email and name are saved only in your own browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to MarketStats or to any server, and we can't see it. You can delete it any time by signing out and clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Cookies, storage and third parties

Your light/dark theme choice and sign-in session are stored in your browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

MarketStats is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (preview copies on GitHub Pages). Fonts and code libraries are served from our own site. Map tiles load from OpenFreeMap, which, like any web server, receives your IP address and browser details when your browser requests a tile. Our host also keeps standard server logs.

Once live accounts are on: what we'll collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you give it, your name.
  • Sign-in records: when you signed in, and basic technical data needed to keep your account secure (such as IP address and browser type).
  • Preferences: settings such as watchlists or alerts, if you use those features later.
  • Payments (paid plans only, when they launch): handled by a payment provider. We won't store your full card number.

We only collect what we need to run MarketStats (APP 3). You can browse the headline map and previews without giving us anything.

How we'll use it

  • To create and secure your account and send sign-in links.
  • To send service emails, such as security notices or changes to these terms.
  • To send data-release updates or marketing only if you opt in. Every marketing email will include an unsubscribe link, as the Spam Act 2003 requires.
  • To understand overall usage so we can improve the site, using aggregated data where possible.

We won't sell your personal information.

Sharing and overseas storage

We use Supabase to store accounts, waitlist sign-ups and contact messages, and to send sign-in emails. Our Supabase project is hosted in Sydney, though the provider and its sub-processors may handle some data outside Australia (APP 8). We'll take reasonable steps to make sure any service provider handles your information consistently with the APPs. We'll only disclose your information otherwise if you agree to it or the law requires it.

Storage and security

We'll take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access (APP 11). That includes encrypted connections, access controls and passwordless sign-in links. We'll delete or de-identify account data when it's no longer needed, or within a reasonable time after you close your account. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you (APPs 12 and 13). You can ask us to update or delete your account, waitlist entry or messages using the contact form on the About page. In demo mode, everything is already in your own browser.

Questions and complaints

Contact us using the form on the About page. If you're not happy with how we handle a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
